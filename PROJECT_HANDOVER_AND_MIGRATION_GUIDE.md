## 1. Project Mission & Origin Story

### 1.1 Original Goal
The primary objective of this project was to construct an image classification system that could accurately distinguish **AI-generated images (synthetic media)** from **authentic camera photographs (real images)** under a strict self-imposed constraint:
> **Strict Constraint:** Zero pretrained models, zero pretrained weights (no `timm`, no `torchvision.models`, no Hugging Face pretrained vision transformers). Everything had to be designed, initialized with random weights, and trained strictly from scratch.

### 1.2 Initial Starting Point
The project began with a single paired dataset of approximately 79,950 images (50% real photographs from Shutterstock, 50% AI-generated images generated by an older single diffusion model `DeepMedia v1`). All training images were standardized to `768x768` JPEG with Quality Factor ~75 (`4:2:0` chroma subsampling).

### 1.3 The User's Experience
- When trained on this dataset, the model achieved stellar training and validation metrics: **97%–99% validation accuracy and F1 score**.
- **The Critical Breakdown:** As soon as the user tested the model on images from the real world—such as photos from their own phone, images generated by modern AI tools (Midjourney v6, FLUX.1, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion 3, ChatGPT, etc.), or images downloaded from the web—the detector failed completely.
- On almost every single real-world AI image, the model predicted **99.9% to 100% "Real Camera Photograph"**.
- The user brought in pairing augmentations, non-AI frequency features, and extensive dataset additions, but the from-scratch paradigm continued to exhibit extreme fragility when facing modern out-of-domain AI generators.

## 2. The Core Failure Mode: Why "From Scratch" Failed in the Real World

Understanding why the detector failed is essential for anyone picking up this project. There are three interconnected reasons:

### 2.1 The "Single-Generator Hyperplane Shortcut"
When a binary classifier is trained from scratch on `Real Camera` vs. `Generator A`:
$$\mathcal{L} = \text{BCE}(f_\theta(x), y)$$
The model has no prior knowledge of what a "cat", "car", "face", or "lens flare" is. Its optimization objective is simply to minimize loss by finding the most discriminative mathematical signals in the pixel grid.
Because `Generator A` has specific micro-patterns (such as a characteristic checkerboard deconvolution pattern, high-frequency spectral spike, or specific color variance floor), the optimizer quickly latches onto that exact mathematical footprint.
The model learns the decision rule:
$$\text{If } \text{Footprint}(x) \approx \text{Footprint}(\text{Gen } A) \implies \text{AI (1)}$$
$$\text{Else} \implies \text{Real (0)}$$

When the user feeds in an image from Midjourney v6, FLUX, or DALL-E 3:
1. The new image does **not** have Generator A's footprint.
2. Therefore, by the model's logic, it belongs to "Everything Else" $\implies$ **100% Real Photograph**.

### 2.2 Internet Laundering (Compression, Resizing, and Messaging)
The user noted:
> *"fresh ai generated image is not same as compressed and sent and then downloaded images of ai, i mean if you just take pure ai generated image it will still learn pattern of that engine, then if i take real ai generated image from internet and test we might still get bad results."*

This is 100% correct and is known in academic forensics as **Social Media Laundering**:
1. **Chroma Subsampling (`4:2:0`):** Platforms like WhatsApp, Instagram, Reddit, and Twitter subsample color components to half-resolution.
2. **DCT Re-Quantization:** JPEG compression rounds DCT coefficients, destroying subtle micro-texture residuals and sensor noise floors.
3. **Resizing / Resampling:** Downscaling or upscaling interpolates pixels (Bilinear/Bicubic/Lanczos), erasing Bayer color filter array (CFA) alignment and high-frequency spectral peaks.
4. **Screenshots & Re-encoding:** A user screenshottng an AI image or saving it as a PNG re-wraps compressed artifacts into a new container, confusing low-level spatial filter banks.

From-scratch convolutional filters that rely on micro-level pixel noise collapse once an image has been forwarded through a messaging app or uploaded to social media.

### 2.3 The "Semantic Prior Deficit"
Human beings and modern foundation models do not identify AI images solely by counting high-frequency noise pixels. We look at **semantics and physical coherence**:
- Hands with six fingers or unnatural joints
- Inconsistent lighting directions, impossible shadows, or mismatched reflections in eyes/mirrors
- Hyper-smooth, plastic skin textures lacking natural pores and subsurface scattering
- Hallucinated text, nonsensical logos, or garbled background details
- Surreal depth-of-field and unnatural semantic context

A network trained from scratch on 70,000 images **cannot learn high-level semantic reality**. It has not seen the billions of diverse images needed to understand physical optics, anatomy, and scene geometry.

## 3. Chronology of Architectures & Engineering Implemented

Over several iterations, this repository developed complex, custom architectures to test the limits of from-scratch forensics:

### ArtifactNet v1 & v2
- **Concept:** Dual-stream network with Spatial branch (EfficientNet-style inverted bottleneck blocks) and Frequency branch (DCT / 2D-FFT magnitude).
- **Issue Discovered:** Synthetic decoder artifacts were generated on real images to simulate fake noise, but this taught the model that smartphone ISP sharpening (e.g. from an iPhone or Samsung) was "AI". Upscaling a real photo caused the model to flag it as 92% AI (Real specificity collapsed to 8%).

### ArtifactNet v4
- **Architecture:** 4-stream network with 10.87M parameters:
  - *Stream 1 (Differential Micro-Residuals):* NPR, Local Phase Discrepancy (LPD), SRM, and zero-sum L1-constrained Bayar convolutions comparing Rich vs. Poor texture patches within the same image (PatchCraft concept).
  - *Stream 2 (Wavelet & Phase Coherence):* 3-level Haar wavelets + 2D-RFFT phase gradients.
  - *Stream 3 (Global Physics & ViT):* ConvNeXt stages + 64-patch non-local Self-Consistency Vision Transformer.
  - *Stream 4 (Gram Covariance):* Differential channel covariance + 24 dimensionless physical ratios.
  - *8-Prototype Real-Camera Manifold on $\mathbb{S}^{255}$:* Attempted to model camera modes as clusters on a hypersphere to detect AI as out-of-manifold anomalies.
- **Outcome:** Reached 97.99% clean validation F1 and 97.51% perturbed F1 on the training dataset split, but still predicted 86.7% "Real" on the 5,540-image `test_data_v2` benchmark because the training set remained dominated by a single generator.

### ArtifactNet v5
- **Architecture:** 16.74M parameters, zero pretrained weights:
  - *Expanded Physics Extractor:* 28 dimensionless & meso-scale physical descriptors (`src/forensics.py`), including $8\times8$ block boundary step ratios, meso-acutance decay ratios, and radial lens sharpness falloff.
  - *16-Prototype Real-Camera Manifold:* Exact non-negative minimum cosine distance $d_{\text{min}}$ on $\mathbb{S}^{255}$ with empirical boundary alignment.
  - *12-Class Generator Family Head:* Explicitly predicts which generative architecture family generated the image (FLUX, OpenAI/DALL-E, Midjourney, Google/Imagen, SD3, SDXL, SD 1/2, Commercial Frontier, Open DiT, AI Edits/Video, Real Camera).
  - *Pair-Synchronized Internet Laundering Loss:* Trains twin $(Real_k, AI_k)$ pairs through identical 1-hop and 2-hop degradation pipelines.
- **Results:**
  - Evaluated on strictly unseen frontier generators (`GPT-Image 1.5`, `Midjourney v7`, `FLUX.2-Klein`, `Z-Image-Turbo`, `Ernie-Image`, in-the-wild `Reddit`):
    - **71.73% Accuracy / 68.86% F1 / 80.35% ROC-AUC**.
    - Successfully caught 82.3% of Ernie-Image, 70.7% of Z-Image-Turbo, 70.3% of GPT-Image 1.5, and 66.6% of Reddit AI uploads.
    - Robustness across 6 internet-laundering stress tests (WhatsApp, 2-hop web, WebP CDN, screenshot PNG, phone ISP, FFT knockout) maintained ~65%–70% F1.
  - Predictions on unlabeled `test_data_v2` (5,540 images) shifted from 86.7% Real / 13.3% AI to **55.6% Real / 44.4% AI**.
- **Conclusion:** While ArtifactNet v5 improved generalization significantly compared to v1–v4, a ~71% accuracy ceiling on unseen real-world data proves that **from-scratch architectures cannot reach the 90%+ reliability required for production deployment**.

## 4. Dataset & Data Engineering Evolution

### Initial Dataset
- Located in `data/train.csv` (79,950 images) and `data/test.csv` (5,540 images).
- Metadata: `id`, `file_name`, `label` (0 = Real, 1 = AI).
- Images reside in `data/train/` and `data/test_data_v2/`.

### The Automated Multi-Generator Harvester (`src/harvest_dataset.py`)
To break the single-generator trap, an automated harvester was created that streams and unpacks Parquet shards directly from Hugging Face into `data/multigen_data/` without filling disk cache:
- **Direct HTTP Range Chunking:** Bypasses Hugging Face local cache bloat (`~/.cache/huggingface/hub`) to preserve SSD space.
- **Sources Harvested:**
  - `ComplexDataLab/OpenFake`: Shuffled multi-generator benchmark containing 57+ generators (FLUX.1/2, GPT-Image, Midjourney v6/v7, DALL-E 3, SD 3.5, SDXL, Imagen, Ideogram, Seedream, Grok, Qwen, Kolors) and real photos from Pexels and LAION.
  - `Rajarshi-Roy-research/Defactify_Image_Dataset`: Real MS-COCO photographs + SD 2.1, SDXL, SD 3, DALL-E 3, and Midjourney v6.
  - `OwensLab/CommunityForensics-Eval`: Camera benchmarking sets (VISION smartphone cameras, Forchheim, IMD2020, FFHQ, landscapes).
  - `Reddit & Civitai 2026 Supplements`: Real-world community uploads with natural user compression.
- **Harvested Result:** 38,714 clean, verified, deduplicated images stored in `data/multigen_data/` across 93 distinct generators and camera sources.
- **Splits Generated:** Located in `data/splits_v5/`:
  - `train_split.csv` (71,676 rows)
  - `val_id_split.csv` (2,000 rows)
  - `val_ood_split.csv` (2,138 rows - strictly unseen generators)
  - `test_ood_split.csv` (2,614 rows - strictly unseen generators)

### Internet Laundering Pipeline & Augmentations (`src/dataset.py`)
To prevent the model from relying on container format or compression level:
- **`sample_internet_laundering_params()` & `apply_internet_laundering()`:**
  - *Regime 1: Clean (22%):* Uncompressed/PNG pass-through with sub-8x8 crop offsets.
  - *Regime 2: 1-Hop Social (30%):* WhatsApp/Instagram/Twitter compression (JPEG Q70–86 `4:2:0` or WebP Q75–90, resizing to 640–1280px, platform unsharp mask).
  - *Regime 3: 2-Hop Laundered (32%):* Upload $\to$ compress $\to$ download $\to$ resize $\to$ forward/save as PNG (simulating saving a web image).
  - *Regime 4: Harsh Web / Screenshot (16%):* Heavy downscaling (480–720px), severe JPEG quantization (Q52–71), screen gamma shift.
- Applied **symmetrically** to matched Real/AI pairs in `BalancedMultiGenPairDataset`.

## 5. The Scientific Post-Mortem: Why Pretrained Foundation Models Are Mandatory

If you are the incoming AI agent, **do not attempt to continue training from scratch**. Here is why:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        THE DUAL-REPRESENTATION DILEMMA                        |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                               |
|  FROM-SCRATCH MODEL:                                                          |
|  [70k Images] --> Learns Basic Edges & Textures --> Latches onto Gen-A Noise  |
|                   ❌ No semantic understanding of physics or anatomy          |
|                   ❌ Noise destroyed by WhatsApp/JPEG compression            |
|                   ❌ Result: 100% "Real" on any unseen AI image               |
|                                                                               |
|  PRETRAINED FOUNDATION MODEL (CLIP / DINOv2 / SigLIP):                        |
|  [1B+ Images] --> Robust Semantic Invariants + Natural World Physics          |
|                   ✅ Detects surreal geometry, warped hands, plastic skin     |
|                   ✅ Survives JPEG compression, downscaling & forwarding      |
|                   ✅ Generalizes across ALL generator architectures           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Pretrained Vision Transformers already possess the "World Model":** Models like `DINOv2` (self-supervised on 142M images) or `CLIP` / `SigLIP` (contrastive text-image on 1B+ pairs) have internal representations of natural lighting, human anatomy, textures, depth, and material properties.
2. **AI Artifacts at High Resolution are Semantic, Not Just Pixel Noise:** Modern diffusion (FLUX, SD3) and autoregressive (GPT-Image) generators produce clean, noise-free outputs. Their mistakes are in **coherence**: unnatural specular highlights, plastic skin transitions, asymmetric eyes, inconsistent shadows. Only models with pretrained semantic backbones can detect these.
3. **Robustness to Compression:** While JPEG compression erases 90% of high-frequency pixel noise, it preserves semantic structure. A pretrained model analyzing semantic anomalies remains accurate even after severe WhatsApp or Reddit compression.

## 6. Blueprint for the New AI Agent: How to Build the SOTA Detector

Here is the exact strategy the new AI agent should follow to build a world-class AI vs. Real image detector:

### 6.1 Top Architectural Paradigms (2024–2026)

#### Paradigm A: The Dual-Branch Foundation Architecture (Recommended First Choice)
- **Branch 1 (Semantic / Global Optics):** Pretrained `dinov2_large` or `clip-vit-large-patch14-336`. Freeze the lower 75% of layers; fine-tune upper layers or attach a LoRA adapter (rank 16, alpha 32).
- **Branch 2 (High-Frequency / Residuals):** Pretrained `convnext_base` or `swin_base` taking SRM-filtered or high-pass residual inputs to capture sensor noise and resampling traces.
- **Fusion:** Cross-attention layer or Gated MLP combining global semantic features ($1024d$) and frequency features ($768d$).

#### Paradigm B: UnivFD / Universal Fake Detection (Simplest & Extremely Strong)
- Based on academic findings (Ojha et al., CVPR; Wang et al.):
- Take a frozen `CLIP-ViT-L/14` (or `DINOv2-ViT-L/14`).
- Extract the penultimate transformer layer tokens (`CLS` token + pooled patch tokens).
- Train a 3-layer MLP classification head with LayerNorm, Dropout (0.3), and GeLU.
- Train with aggressive data augmentations (JPEG Q30–Q95, Blur, Resize).
- *Why it works:* CLIP's feature space naturally clusters synthetic images away from real photography without catastrophic forgetting.

#### Paradigm C: DIRE / Diffusion Reconstruction Error (For Pure Diffusion Detection)
- Pass the input image through a lightweight pretrained diffusion model (e.g. DDIM inversion on Stable Diffusion 1.5 or SDXL).
- Calculate the reconstruction residual:
$$\text{DIRE}(x) = |x - R(x)|$$
- AI-generated diffusion images have significantly smaller reconstruction error than real photographs because they lie closer to the diffusion manifold.
- Train a lightweight classifier (e.g. ResNet50 or ConvNeXt-Tiny) on $\text{DIRE}(x)$.

### 6.4 Essential Data Augmentation Regimes

To ensure the new model does not break when images are shared over WhatsApp, Telegram, or social media:

```python
import albumentations as A

train_transform = A.Compose([
    A.RandomResizedCrop(height=336, width=336, scale=(0.6, 1.0), p=1.0),
    A.HorizontalFlip(p=0.5),
    A.ImageCompression(quality_lower=35, quality_upper=95, p=0.8),  # WhatsApp/Instagram
    A.OneOf([
        A.GaussianBlur(blur_limit=(3, 7), p=1.0),
        A.MotionBlur(blur_limit=(3, 7), p=1.0),
        A.Defocus(radius=(1, 3), p=1.0),
    ], p=0.4),
    A.ColorJitter(brightness=0.15, contrast=0.15, saturation=0.15, hue=0.05, p=0.5),
    A.Downscale(scale_min=0.4, scale_max=0.9, interpolation=cv2.INTER_AREA, p=0.4),
    A.Normalize(mean=[0.485, 0.456, 0.406], std=[0.229, 0.224, 0.225]),
])
```

### 6.5 Step-by-Step Execution Plan for the New Agent

1. **Setup & Clean Baseline (Hours 1–2):**
   - Create a clean script `train_pretrained.py`.
   - Load `timm.create_model('vit_large_patch14_clip_336', pretrained=True, num_classes=2)`.
   - Freeze all backbone parameters except the final layer and classification head.
   - Train on `data/splits_v5/train_split.csv` using AdamW ($lr = 10^{-4}$, cosine schedule, 5 epochs).
   - Evaluate on `data/splits_v5/test_ood_split.csv` and compare against ArtifactNet v5's 71.7% baseline. (Expectation: 85%–92% immediate OOD accuracy).

2. **Add LoRA Fine-Tuning (Hours 3–4):**
   - Apply `peft.LoraConfig` to the attention query/value projection matrices of the ViT backbone (`r=16, lora_alpha=32`).
   - Fine-tune with lower learning rate ($lr = 3 \times 10^{-5}$) for 6–8 epochs.
   - Measure performance across the 6 internet-laundering stress tests (`src/evaluate.py`).

3. **Multi-Model Ensemble / Dual-Branch (Hours 5–6):**
   - Combine predictions from `CLIP-ViT-L/14` (semantic/textual prior) and `DINOv2-ViT-L/14` (dense visual patch prior).
   - Simple average or linear logistic blend of logits yields state-of-the-art generalizability.

4. **Export & Deployment:**
   - Update `app.py` to run the new pretrained model with the Gradio UI.
   - Export weights to `hf_export/model.safetensors`.
